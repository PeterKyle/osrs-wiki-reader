        Quick guide

        Get set up in a minute, plus the keys worth knowing.

        Set up

        1. Your accountClick the person icon, look yourself up and press This is me. Quest and skill pages then tick off what you meet, and the Wise Old Man knows your levels.
        2. The Wise Old Man (AI helper)Click his face in the toolbar and connect a free Google Gemini key. It takes about a minute; see How to get a free Gemini key below.
        3. HotkeysOpen Settings (the gear) and click a hotkey to change it, if it clashes with your game or other apps.
        How to get a free Gemini key
        1. Open Google AI Studio and sign in with your Google (Gmail) account. Accept the terms if it asks.
        2. Click Create API key. If it asks for a project, pick the suggested one or create a new one.
        3. In the list of keys, check its tier says Free.
        4. Copy the key and paste it into the Wise Old Man chat.

        Keep it free: in AI Studio, never click Set up billing or Upgrade, and never add a card. A key without billing can't be charged; if you use up the free amount, it just pauses until the next day. If Google won't give you a key without adding billing, stop there.

        Tips

        • Highlight text on any page and press Ask about this to ask the Wise Old Man about it.
        • The chat docks beside the page; drag its left edge to resize. The clock button keeps your last 10 chats.
        • Drag the top bar to the top of the screen for Windows' snap layouts. Double-click it to maximize.
        • The pin keeps the reader above your game, and mini mode shrinks it to a small search bar.
        • Item pages show live Grand Exchange prices, and Alert tells you when a price crosses your target.
        • Quest and diary ticks need the free WikiSync plugin in RuneLite.

        Open this again any time: press F1, or Settings → Quick guide.
